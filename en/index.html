What is Grey?

Grey gives you foreign bank accounts you can link to PayPal and receive payments on (if they comply with Grey's terms), plus a virtual card for paying online subscriptions.

Ways to fund your Grey account

There are three options. Pick what you have access to:

1

PayPal

Link your Grey USD bank account to PayPal, then receive transfers or withdraw from PayPal to Grey.

2

Direct bank transfer

Send money straight from another account to your Grey bank details (EUR or USD), as long as your bank supports those transfers and ACH.

3

Crypto

If you have no PayPal or other bank accounts, this is the route. It's covered in detail below.

Top up with crypto (lowest fees)

The idea: choose the network with the lowest minimum and fees. In my testing, USDC on BEP20 was the best fit. Grey supports two coins here: USDC and USDT.

Network fees and minimums in the table are Bybit's, taken from my tutorial, not from Grey, and may change. Grey officially states it accepts USDC/USDT depending on your country; available networks appear in the app under Add money → Stablecoins.

  1. In Grey: choose USDC, pick the BEP20 network, and copy the deposit address.
  2. On Bybit: open Assets, then Withdraw, then crypto withdrawal.
  3. If your balance is USDT or another coin, convert it to USDC first (for example $7), set the price, and confirm.
  4. Paste the address, double-check it, and select the exact same network shown in Grey.
  5. Enter the amount. For a $5 card, send $6 or $7 to cover fees.
  6. Tap withdraw and confirm via email and Google Authenticator.
  7. Track it in your transaction history. Once complete, refresh Grey and the balance appears.
Always make sure the network and address match on both platforms. A wrong network can mean lost funds.

Issue and manage the virtual card

After funding, open the Cards section and pick your card. A details page lists all its features before you pay.

Card issuing cost

  • Visa: needs $5 in your balance.
  • Mastercard: costs $4, plus $1 loaded onto the card ($5 total).

Limits (per Grey Help Center)

Card dashboard

  1. Open “Details” and enter your app PIN to reveal the card number, CVV and expiry date.
  2. A ready-made billing address is provided, usually a US one, along with its ZIP code.
  3. The Visa card is linked directly to your wallet and can't be topped up separately. If your wallet isn't USD, the amount is converted at payment time with a conversion fee. The Mastercard is funded from the app.
  4. “Card Limits” shows spending limits, and “Card Label” lets you name your card.
  5. There's a points system: spend e.g. $200 within 90 days to earn a bonus offer.
Security tip: freeze the card right after every online payment. If someone gets your card data, they can't use it until you unfreeze it yourself.

Link the card to Google Play

Then you can pay for app subscriptions (like ChatGPT) through the Google Play store.

  1. In Google Play tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Payment methods → Add credit or debit card.
  2. Enter the card number, expiry and security code; copy the cardholder name from the Grey app.
  3. Choose the country: if yours is Egypt, switch it to the United States (or whatever the card's address shows).
  4. Enter the ZIP code from the same details page, then save the card.
  5. If the card doesn't show up at checkout, close and reopen Google Play.
Use the card details and billing address exactly as Grey shows them. Don't type your own.

Important notes from experience

Free trials

Platforms are strict with first-month free trials, and the card may well be declined there.

ChatGPT test

In my test the card was accepted when paying directly for a paid plan, but not for the free trial.

Small deductions

I noticed a 72-cent deduction on one transaction, so review your crypto balance and convert what you need.

Add a little extra

Fund slightly more than the subscription price (e.g. $6 or $7 for a $5 plan) to cover fees.

Official information from Grey

I checked these points against Grey's official help center on November 8, 2026. If the app ever differs, the app is the reference.

Fees

No card maintenance, transaction or funding fees. One-time creation fee: Visa $5, Mastercard $4 (+$1 credited to the card balance).

Non-US payment fee

A fee of up to 2% + $0.50 may apply on non-US websites. It is charged by the card network, not Grey, and also applies to refunds.

Negative balance

If the card balance goes negative and reaches $50, the card is terminated automatically. Keep enough funds before paying.

Refunds

Refunds are settled in your USD balance, and conversion fees are not refundable.

Subscriptions

Grey says the card works on most subscription platforms, including ChatGPT Plus. If it's declined over a country mismatch, try adding it through the Play Store or App Store.

Stablecoins

Grey accepts USDC and USDT depending on your country of residence. Under Add money → Stablecoins you get the wallet address.

Frequently asked questions

Does the Grey card work with ChatGPT?

Grey says the card works on most subscription platforms, including ChatGPT Plus. In my test it was accepted when paying directly for a paid plan, while free trials may be declined.

How much does a Grey card cost to create?

Visa costs $5 and Mastercard costs $4 (+$1 credited to the card balance). Per Grey's help center there are no maintenance, transaction or funding fees.

What are the Grey card limits?

Visa: $10,000 per day and $50,000 per month. Mastercard: up to $10,000 per transaction, with funding from $2 to $2,500 per transaction.

What is the cheapest way to fund Grey with crypto?

In my testing: USDC on the BEP20 network, with a Bybit withdrawal fee of about $0.20 at recording time. Make sure the network and address match, and check current fees.

Can I link the Grey card to Google Play?

Yes. Add it as a credit or debit card under payment methods, using the exact card details and billing address shown in the Grey app.
